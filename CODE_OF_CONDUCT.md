# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for people of every background, identity, experience level, discipline, and institution. Participation should make it easier for educators and developers to learn from one another.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without condescension.
- Credit ideas, code, and educational content accurately.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Focus criticism on the work and its effect, not on the person.
- Accept correction, repair harm where possible, and move discussions forward.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention
- Personal attacks, trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sharing restricted teaching materials, student information, or confidential reports
- Misrepresenting authorship, permission, endorsement, or affiliation
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels, and public events where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. If the report involves private data or a security issue, follow [Security](SECURITY.md). Do not force an affected person to disclose sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect confidentiality where practicable, and respond proportionately. Possible actions include a private correction, warning, temporary participation limits, removal of content, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest should recuse themselves.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html) and adapted to emphasize educational privacy, authorship, and public-content boundaries.
