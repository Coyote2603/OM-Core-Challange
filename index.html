    About this version

    Built for one course, shared for others to adapt.

    This demonstration uses fictional content to show what the platform can do without sharing original course materials. Every component can be kept, changed, or removed to suit a different context.

    Illustrative content

    Example sessions

    The fictional sessions demonstrate concepts, activities, resources, problems, and presenter-ready slides. They are examples, not a suggested course structure.

    How this version works

    One possible student journey

    In the original platform, explanation, application, and feedback were kept together. The three steps illustrate that flow rather than prescribe it.

    1. 1
      Concepts and objectives

      Objectives, concept cards, and the visual model introduce the topic.

    2. 2
      Examples and discussion

      Interactive slides combine examples, optional hints, and discussion prompts.

    3. 3
      Practice and feedback

      Problems include worked responses that can be revealed after an attempt.
