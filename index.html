    How it works

    Every wrong answer teaches something

    Nothing is timed or auto-advanced. Try any option as many times as you need before moving on.

    1. 1
      Read the scenario

      Each problem states the setup, the numbers, and the question being asked.

    2. 2
      Pick an option

      A wrong choice shows a hint aimed at exactly that misconception, not a generic nudge.

    3. 3
      Find the right one

      The correct option reveals the full worked solution, with every step shown.
