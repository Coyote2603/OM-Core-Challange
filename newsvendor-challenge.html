    The Newsvendor Challenge

    Run a campus newsstand through eight changing mornings. Choose how many copies to order before demand is known, then judge your decision against Q* — the optimal order — on that same morning's actual demand.

    The eight-morning run

    What changes, morning to morning

    After the baseline, five mornings each change exactly one input — mean demand, uncertainty, selling price, unit cost, or salvage value. The seventh combines two changes pulling in opposite directions. The eighth is a capstone: everything moves at once.

    Your mission: maximize total profit across all eight mornings. After each one, your choice is compared with that morning's Q* on the exact same realized demand — not on average, on that draw.
