# Acknowledgments

## Platform and game

The Interactive Teaching Platform and the original Newsvendor Challenge were designed and developed by **Stefanos Poulidis** as a shareable version of tools first built for his students. This adaptation replaces that game with the OM Core Challenge, a six-problem set on capacity, batching, queueing, and process analysis, while keeping the surrounding platform unchanged.

## Teaching lineage

The private tutorial practice that motivated this public infrastructure was shaped over many years by the care and creativity of a broader teaching community. Jiatao Ding, Bengisu, Clara Carrera, and many other outstanding INSEAD PhD colleagues developed and refined exercises that supported that private teaching environment.

Their original exercises, solutions, recordings, and course documents are **not included in this public repository**. This acknowledgment does not imply that those contributors or INSEAD have licensed, endorsed, or approved the synthetic materials published here. The five demo sessions in this repository are newly written examples whose purpose is to show how the infrastructure can be used.

## Reusers and contributors

Thank you to instructors, teaching assistants, learning designers, developers, and students who test the platform and suggest improvements. Public contributions are recognized through the repository's Git history and release notes.
